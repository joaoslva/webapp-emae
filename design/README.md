# Design references

Drop screenshots, links and saves here (Pinterest, dashboard frameworks, anything).
Subfolders are fine — e.g. `data-entry/`, `dashboards/`, `navigation/`.

Worth noting alongside each one, even a single line: **what it's doing well**. "Nice" is
hard to act on; "the row density lets you scan 30 records without scrolling" is a decision
we can copy or reject.

Two things specific to this app that are worth holding in mind while collecting:

- **The data-entry grid is the hard part, not the dashboards.** Teachers are replacing a
  spreadsheet they already know. If entry is slower than Excel, nothing else matters.
  References for dense editable tables are more valuable here than pretty chart layouts.
- **Know the real conditions.** School hardware, possibly old browsers, teachers working
  occasionally and under time pressure. That pushes toward legible and fast over
  impressive — but let's look at the references before deciding how far.
